# MERIDIAN campaign artwork

Three original local product visuals generated for this implementation using the built-in `image_gen` tool. No remote image, font, or paid generation service is required when running MERIDIAN. The images contain no third-party brand logos. Campaign copy, offers, QR destinations and selection still come from the existing API; the artwork is presentation only.

| File                          | Creative direction                                                         |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `little-orbit-expedition.png` | Sculptural toy adventure, coral photography, bold sans-serif headline      |
| `little-orbit-postcards.png`  | Ivory travel-journal flat lay, forest green serif editorial composition    |
| `aurelia-atelier.png`         | Amber fragrance, travertine, architectural light and restrained typography |

The UI uses locally bundled Instrument Sans Variable, licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1. The complete license is in `Instrument-Sans-OFL.txt`. The editorial alternate uses the system serif fallback stack.

## Prompt set

### Little Orbit — Expedition

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: offline hero product artwork for fictional premium children's airport travel retailer Little Orbit. Create a stunning tactile, photorealistic studio still-life: a beautifully designed miniature cream-and-coral wooden passenger airplane banking over a tiny sculpted adventure landscape, forest-green child-size travel backpack, soft golden plush explorer bear with a small scarf, and a curved orange toy road. Sophisticated collectible toy product photography, rich coral-orange background with warm studio shadows and golden side light. Interesting varied heights and energetic diagonal composition, three-quarter camera angle, premium material detail and craftsmanship. Objects fill center and right, clean red-orange negative space at left but no typography. Landscape 3:2 image, crisp large-scale product forms suitable for cropping vertically too. No text, no letters, no blocks, no logos, no watermark, no real brand references. Original campaign artwork, not a UI mockup.

### Aurelia — Atelier

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: premium fictional airport fragrance campaign hero photography for Aurelia. Create an atmospheric editorial luxury perfume still life: a single sculptural unbranded clear amber glass fragrance bottle with oversized dark brown rounded cap, on travertine stone, reflected warm sunlight, a translucent softly rippling curtain behind it, one long architectural shadow. Elegant beauty photography, deep espresso and warm honey palette, tactile glass detail, subtle grain. Bottle prominently in right half with generous dark negative space at left; cinematic landscape 3:2 composition, no text, no logos, no watermark. Sophisticated actual product photography, not a graphic template or UI. Original offline-ready campaign asset.

### Little Orbit — Postcards

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: an alternate original hero photograph for fictional premium children's travel retailer Little Orbit. Create a beautifully art-directed overhead editorial flat lay of an unbranded mint-green and butter-yellow miniature toy airplane, a small open illustrated adventure activity journal with drawings but no writing, colored pencils, playful animal-shaped wooden travel toys, and a tiny soft orange explorer backpack. Rich tactile paper and fabric, soft window shadows, warm ivory photographic background, joyful but sophisticated color palette of forest green, pastel mint, butter yellow, orange. Real product photography with a handmade travel journal mood; objects arranged across left and center with generous ivory space on the right for advertising copy that will be added in code. Landscape 3:2 composition. No readable text, no letters, no building blocks, no logos, no watermark. Clearly distinct from a dramatic red-orange studio still-life.
